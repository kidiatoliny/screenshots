Tycho: the inspector saves first.

An inspector loses edits in more ways than closing it. Switching task, changing layout, opening a new-task sheet, closing the window. Each one now saves the title, estimate and description you were typing first. A draft for a task that a data reset removed is skipped instead of bringing the task back.
