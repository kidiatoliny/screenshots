## Text: LinkedIn

One of the oldest debugging tricks still works: explain the problem out loud.

Walk through the code line by line, to a coworker or to a rubber duck on your desk. You have to slow down and say what each line actually does, not what you think it does.

Very often the bug shows up before you finish explaining.
