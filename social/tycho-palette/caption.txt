Tycho: press Command-K and go anywhere.

The palette opens with suggestions, recent tasks and navigation. Type and it finds tasks and projects, grouped by scope; View all opens a search page with a count per scope. Arrow keys, Return, Tab and Escape drive it. Single keys and G chords navigate too, and they stand down while you type, while a sheet is open, and during onboarding.

Swipe → for the key points.
Code → https://github.com/akira-foundation/tycho
