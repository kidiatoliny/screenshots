The hard part of Cabo Verde's e-Fatura, as a Node.js package.

@akira-io/efatura validates the fiscal document, generates the DFE XML, packages the ZIP and signs it with XAdES-BES. Adapters for Express, Fastify and Nest. Still in beta.
