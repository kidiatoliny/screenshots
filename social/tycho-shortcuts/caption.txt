Tycho: keyboard shortcuts that stay out of the way.

Press ? and every key is on one sheet. N, P and / need no Command key, and G starts a chord: G then H goes Home, G then T to My Tasks. In a text field N is just the letter N, and with a sheet open nothing fires behind it. Onboarding was the last leak: a shortcut pressed before the workspace existed used to fire once it did. Now a new workspace drops it and opens on Home.

Swipe → for the key points.
