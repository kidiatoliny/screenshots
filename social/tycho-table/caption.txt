Tycho: rename a task right in the row.

Double-click a title in the table view and it becomes a text field. Status, priority, assignee, dates, labels, dependencies and estimate edit inline too. Columns sort, resize and hide, and each view keeps its own. On iPhone the rows become cards.

Swipe → for the key points.
