## Text: LinkedIn

Akira Debugger is a desktop app that receives the ray() calls from your app and shows each one the way it should look: an executed query, a mailable as the mail, an Eloquent model with its attributes, an exception with its location and a click to open that line in your editor.

Entries can carry a label and a colour, and you can filter by type or colour, search, expand or collapse everything, keep the window on top, and export the log. It runs on macOS (universal) and Windows.
