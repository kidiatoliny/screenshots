# Unified Dev: quick search panel

Planned: X 16/12 18:00, Bluesky 16/12 18:00 (Europe/Luxembourg)

## Text: X

Unified Dev's quick search panel now uses the system's own controls: the scope filters are a segmented picker, keys are printed the way the menu bar prints them, escape shows as its glyph, and the selected row takes your accent colour.

## Text: Bluesky

Unified Dev's quick search panel now uses the system's own controls: a segmented picker for the scopes, keys printed like the menu bar prints them, and the selected row in your accent colour.
