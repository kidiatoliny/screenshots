# Unified Dev: side conversations

Planned: X 25/12 10:00, Bluesky 25/12 10:00 (Europe/Luxembourg)

## Text: X

In Unified Dev you can open a side conversation in the inspector, next to the chat, to ask something without derailing the main one. Closing it now really ends it: before, a Reply after a close brought the old one back, quote and all.
