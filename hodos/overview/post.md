## Text: LinkedIn

I'm building Akira Hodos, a public transport app for iPhone.

There are several transit apps here, but the ones I found work in Luxembourg or in France, and each has its limits. I wanted one app that works wherever I travel. Today it covers both countries: Luxembourg is always on, and France is one switch away in Settings.

You plan a journey between stops, addresses and places, with live times, delays and platform numbers in every result. A map shows the stops around you with their next departures, and the buses and trams as they move. Once you are on your way, the trip shows on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

It also saves the routes you repeat, tells you about disruptions on the routes you use, and keeps statistics on your own commute.

There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. Your saved routes and trip history stay on your iPhone.

It is coming soon to the App Store.
