# Akira Hodos: favourites and stats

Planned: X 27/11 18:00, Bluesky 27/11 18:00 (Europe/Luxembourg)

## Text: X

Akira Hodos saves the routes you repeat and keeps stats on your own commute: time spent on transit, how often you arrived on time, when you travel. No account, no analytics. It all stays on the iPhone.
