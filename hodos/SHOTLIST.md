# Hodos: shot list

Source: the Hodos iOS app (private, Xcode project). Store name: Akira Hodos.
How to shoot: these are the app's own App Store screenshots, iPhone 6.9 inch, 1320x2868, light mode, demo data (Luxembourg, Gare Centrale to Belval). They are copied here as they are, never cropped or edited. On a slide an iPhone shot sits whole inside a bezel.

Screens and their status. The post that uses a feature's shots goes in that feature's folder as `post.md` (written by Claude).

- [x] `overview/01-plan.png`: Plan Journey, Luxembourg, Gare Centrale to Belval (Université), 6 routes found, 46 min.
- [x] `overview/02-nearby.png`: Nearby, the map of stops around the user.
- [x] `overview/03-live-vehicles.png`: Live vehicles, buses and trams on the map labelled by line.
- [x] `overview/04-departures.png`: a stop's platforms and next departures over the Nearby map.
- [x] `overview/05-itinerary.png`: the itinerary 14:10 to 14:56 with every stop, platforms, delays and "Tracks automatically once you're on this route".
- [x] `overview/06-route-map.png`: the route on the map, "Arrives 14:56 +4 min".
- [x] `overview/07-disruptions.png`: Disruptions, tabs "Affecting me" and "All".
- [x] `overview/08-favorites.png`: Favorites, four saved routes.
