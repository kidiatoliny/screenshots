## Text: LinkedIn

The best transit app is the one you don't have to open.

In Akira Hodos, the public transport app I'm building for iPhone, once you're on a route the trip moves to the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island. You glance at the phone instead of unlocking it, finding the app and waiting for it to load while the bus is pulling in.

It works in Luxembourg and, with one switch, in France. No account, no analytics, no ads.

It's coming soon to the App Store.
