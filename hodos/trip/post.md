## Text: LinkedIn

Following a trip shouldn't need any taps.

In Akira Hodos, the public transport app I'm building for iPhone, you pick one of the routes it found and get the itinerary: every stop, its platform, live delays. The map shows the route and when you'll arrive, "Arrives 14:56, +4 min". Once you're on it, it tracks the trip on its own, and the trip shows on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

It works in Luxembourg and, with one switch in Settings, in France. No account, no analytics, no ads.

How do you get around where you live?
