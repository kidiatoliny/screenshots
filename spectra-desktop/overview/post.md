## Text: LinkedIn

Most API clients start from an empty request. Spectra starts from your project.

Point it at a Laravel app and it reads the routes from artisan. Point it at a NestJS app and it reads them from the source, without running it, including the global prefix and the DTOs. Either way the endpoints show up grouped by resource, with method and path, ready to send.

What you keep in it stays on your machine: environments, request history and saved accounts (bearer tokens, passwords, API keys, TOTP, cookies), encrypted, in a separate store for each person who signs in.
