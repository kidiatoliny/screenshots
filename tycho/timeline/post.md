# Tycho: the timeline

Planned: 2026-11-18 10:00 Luxembourg (X + Bluesky)

## Text: X

Tycho's timeline: task bars you drag to shift dates, arrows for dependencies, milestones, and grouping by status, project or assignee, in weeks or months. The calendar works the same way: drag a task to another day.
