# Tycho: files on tasks

Planned: 2026-11-17 10:00 Luxembourg (X + Bluesky)

## Text: X

Tasks in Tycho can hold files: drop them in or use the picker, up to 250 MB each, in a grid or a list. Quick Look previews them, and rename, duplicate and delete are a click away.
