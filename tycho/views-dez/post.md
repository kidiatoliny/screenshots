## Text: LinkedIn

One project, five ways to look at it.

In Tycho, the task manager I'm building in SwiftUI, a project can be a board, a list, a table, a calendar or a timeline. They're the same tasks, so moving a card on the board or a bar on the timeline changes the same data.

The timeline is the one I use most when planning: it draws the dependency arrows between tasks and the milestones, so you see what is blocking what before it blocks you. The board is for the day to day.

Which view do you live in?
