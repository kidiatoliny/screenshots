# Tycho: the inspector saves first

Planned: 2026-11-18 08:30 Luxembourg (LinkedIn)

## Text: LinkedIn

A small lesson from building Tycho, my SwiftUI task manager.

The task inspector holds edits you haven't saved yet: a title, an estimate, a description. I thought closing it was the moment to save. It's one of many.

The inspector also goes away when you switch to another task, when the layout changes (on a narrow Mac window or a compact iPhone width it becomes a sheet), when you open a new-task sheet, and when the window closes. Each of those now saves the draft first. And a draft for a task that a data reset removed is skipped, instead of bringing the task back.

What's the sneakiest way your app lost a user's typing?
